Imagery: NASA GIBS Blue Marble · Elevation: AWS Terrarium DEM · Rivers: Natural Earth 10m vectors. Land is split into Grassland/Forest/Hill/Mountain using colour + elevation; rivers are vector data drawn as overlays on whatever terrain they cross. Equal-area projection preserves area (every hex ≈ equal Earth area); equidistant preserves true distance & direction from the centre. No single projection is perfectly both — switch to compare.
